This is all quite funny although I should underline that the humor of Don Quixote is not limited to linguistic pastiche. The humor is as “lowbrow” as “high-minded.” Quixote and his squire Sancho are physically injured in slapstick sequences out of The Three Stooges and at times gross-out comedy you might expect from South Park:

One might reply that this humor is less like Stooges or South Park and more like his contemporary Shakespeare.4 Quite true. But since I read Shakespeare untranslated, his language remains archaic and (at least for me) requires a second of deciphering that makes even gutter groundling humor land in my head rather than my gut. In this way, Cervantes is making me understand how Shakespeare’s humor landed in its time.
